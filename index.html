Creator–Brand
Collaboration Agreement

Between Kamani Brown (Creator) — youtube.com/@KamaniBrown · kamanibrown@gmail.com — and the Brand named in the Deal Schedule below. Fill in the Deal Schedule, sign, and download your PDF copy. This is a working template, not legal advice.

1. Deal Schedule (completed per collaboration)

Each collaboration is governed by this Agreement plus the Deal Schedule below. If the Deal Schedule and this Agreement conflict, the Deal Schedule governs for that collaboration. The media kit is the menu — packages, add-ons, and prices. This Agreement is what makes the selection binding: the package and add-ons recorded here determine what Brand is licensed to do, and anything beyond that selection is not included.

* Required before signing. Dates may be left as text if a window rather than a fixed day is agreed.

2. Services

2.1 Creator will produce the deliverable(s) in the Deal Schedule in Creator's own style, voice, and format. Brand may provide a brief, talking points, and product information, but does not direct Creator's opinions, script, or editing choices.

2.2 Creator will disclose the partnership as required by law and platform policy (e.g., #ad / paid promotion labels).

2.3 Brand will ship any product needed for the deliverable at Brand's expense, in time for the agreed dates. Delays in shipping extend Creator's deadlines by the same number of days.

3. Review and approval

3.1 Creator will share a draft (or link) before publication where the Deal Schedule requires it. Brand has 7 calendar days from delivery of the draft to request revisions.

3.2 If Brand does not request revisions within 7 days, the draft is deemed accepted.

3.3 The Fee includes two rounds of revisions for factual errors and brief compliance. Additional rounds, re-shoots, or changes of creative direction are billed separately at a rate agreed in writing.

3.4 Brand may not require Creator to make false claims, misrepresent the product, or remove honest opinions. Creator's review remains Creator's own.

4. Fees and payment — 50% upfront

4.1 Deposit. 50% of the Total Fee is due on signing, before production begins. Production does not start until the deposit has cleared.

4.2 Balance. The remaining 50% is due within 15 calendar days of publication (or of delivery, if publication is delayed by Brand).

4.3 Method. Payment is made by the method stated in the Deal Schedule (e.g., PayPal, wire transfer). All amounts are net: Brand bears transfer, currency-conversion, and processing fees, so Creator receives the full Fee.

4.4 Late fee. Amounts unpaid past their due date accrue a late fee of 2% per month (or part thereof) until paid.

4.5 Invoices. Creator will issue invoices for the deposit and the balance. Brand will pay against the invoice method stated in the Deal Schedule.

4.6 Currency. All fees are in USD.

5. Usage rights and ownership

5.1 Ownership. Creator retains full copyright in all content. Nothing in this Agreement transfers ownership to Brand.

5.2 Licence follows the package. On receipt of the full Fee, Creator grants Brand the licence matching the package and add-ons selected from the media kit and recorded in the Deal Schedule. Unless the Deal Schedule states a broader licence, that licence is:

  • Channels: Brand's own social media accounts and product pages only (organic posting)
  • Territory: worldwide; Term: 12 months from publication

A basic package — a single Short or a single long-form video posted on Creator's channel — carries no broader usage than this unless an add-on says so.

5.3 Paid advertising excluded by default. Use in paid ads, whitelisting, broadcast, retail, or out-of-home is never included by default. It requires a separate usage add-on selected from the media kit and recorded in the Deal Schedule, with its fee agreed in writing before such use.

5.4 No payment, no licence. If the Fee is not paid in full, the licence in §5.2 does not activate. Brand must not use, repost, or run the content until payment is complete.

5.5 Exclusivity. Exclusivity (e.g., not featuring competing products for a period) is never included by default. It is available only as a media-kit add-on recorded in the Deal Schedule, charged additionally.

6. Cancellation and kill fees

6.1 If Brand cancels after signing but before production starts, the deposit is non-refundable.

6.2 If Brand cancels after Creator has delivered a draft or finished filming, Brand owes 100% of the Total Fee.

6.3 If Creator cancels before delivery for reasons within Creator's control, Creator refunds the deposit.

7. Non-payment remedies

7.1 If any amount is more than 30 calendar days overdue, Creator may, after written notice, remove or unlist the deliverable(s) until payment is received, and suspend the licence in §5.2.

7.2 Creator's right to payment survives removal: taking content down does not cancel the debt.

7.3 Each Party bears its own costs of any dispute, unless a court or tribunal orders otherwise.

8. Warranties

8.1 Creator warrants the deliverable(s) are Creator's original work and do not knowingly infringe third-party rights.

8.2 Brand warrants the product is lawful to sell in the markets where the content will be shown, and that product claims supplied to Creator are truthful and substantiated.

8.3 Neither Party guarantees views, sales, or any performance result. Fees are flat and are not contingent on metrics.

9. Term, termination, confidentiality

9.1 This Agreement covers the collaboration in the Deal Schedule. Either Party may terminate for material breach if the breach is not cured within 14 days of written notice.

9.2 Sections 4 (payment), 5 (usage/ownership), 6 (kill fees), and 7 (non-payment) survive termination.

9.3 Neither Party will disclose the other's non-public business information (including fees) except as required by law or to professional advisers.

10. General

10.1 Governing law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of New Brunswick, Canada. The Parties will attempt to resolve disputes by good-faith discussion, then mediation, before any court or tribunal proceeding.

10.2 Entire agreement. This Agreement plus the Deal Schedule is the entire agreement for the collaboration and supersedes prior discussions about it.

10.3 Changes. Changes must be in writing and signed (including electronically) by both Parties.

10.4 Electronic signatures. This Agreement may be signed electronically. Electronic signatures (including typed names, click-to-sign, and drawn signatures captured with a timestamp) are binding to the same extent as handwritten signatures, and a signed PDF copy delivered to each Party is a complete record of the signed Agreement.
